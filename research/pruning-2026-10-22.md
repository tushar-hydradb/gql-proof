# pruning the typed fixed point without losing a label

the goal is totality: for every production and cdrc pair, a valid tree or a
proof that none exists at the cap. `research/totality-2026-10-21.md` rests on
lemma 1, the fixed point is exact, so any saving has to leave every
nonterminal's label set as it is. this note says where the work was, which of it
was redundant, the prunings, the argument and the check that each is exact,
and what is left. the engine is `src/context.rs`, the rule
`src/context/types.rs` under `src/context/scope.rs`; the cost model is
`research/complexity-2026-10-22.md`

two sessions built the same two prunings on the same day without seeing each
other: main got the fold (`a8c0ec1`) and per-pair join keys checked inside the
engine's push (`ae67350`, `--keys`), branch `tushar/prune` the fold and keys
that group a position's child labels once per scan so the pairs ruled out are
never enumerated. measured against each other at two variables they are the
same to the second (48.1 s against 48.8 s): the skipped pair costs a key
lookup, which is nothing next to a push. the merge keeps main's fold and key
definitions and the grouping in the engine's scans and emission passes, plus
the branch's exactness test, the simple CASE operand check at the push of the
when list, and the census and waste counts at any cap

## where the work was

- pushes: a binary expression production pairs every partial label after its
  first operand with every label of its second: `|L_a| · |L_b|`. at two
  variables with joins, the comparison tried 16.9M pairs and simple CASE 32.9M
  (`pair_waste` at `GQL_SLOTS=2 GQL_JOIN=1`)
- most pairs lead nowhere: of the comparison's pairs 20% reach a finished
  label, of simple CASE's 5%; 90% of both are kept by the push and only die at
  finish. so the check came too late, and the pairs that could never pass were
  still enumerated
- partial layers: the partial label after the second operand held both
  operands' classes (`kids`) until finish folded them: 467k partial labels at
  one position of the comparison, 249k at simple CASE, each stored three
  times (itself, the partial before, the option). that was the memory (1.84 GB
  at two variables) and a good part of the time
- the same type work under many scope labels: at `comparison predicate part
  2` the 36 scope labels (120 at two variables) fall into 3 view shapes (6),
  which is all the types rule reads of scope. the type pairs under two scope
  labels of one shape are the same pairs, computed again (`census` with
  `GQL_SLOTS`, `GQL_JOIN`). not removed yet, see below

## the prunings, each exact

1. **joins** (before this branch, `--join`): labels equal but for one
   variable's set of allowed classes become one. one variable: 81,406 labels
   to 24,795, pushes 31.4M to 5.4M. two variables went from no fixed point in
   600 s to one in 101 s

2. **fold at the last classed child** (`Types::fold`, `fold_at`): an
   expression node is evaluated at the push of its last child that carries a
   class, not at finish; the children after it (parentheses, END) carry the
   label, and finish only relinks it. exact because an expression node binds
   no variable: what scope says of every slot is the same after that push and
   after the node's finish, so the classes chosen for deferred slots and the
   needs written are the ones finish would choose. the binders and consumers
   inside the expression closure (`let variable definition`, `value
   initializer`, `return item`: `READS`) stay at finish, since their new
   variable gets its class from scope's finished label. the first version
   folded them too and lost `LET x = 1`; the test caught it

3. **the simple CASE operand at the push of its when list**
   (`case_operand_fits`): 20.7 SR 2 needs the operand's class in every when
   operand's mask; both are there when the when list is pushed, so the pair is
   refused there instead of at END. the same condition, earlier

4. **join keys** (`Context::partial_key`, `child_key`, `compatible`;
   `--keys`): a position's child labels are grouped by their key once per
   scan (`Keyed`), and a partial label is pushed only with the groups
   compatible with its own key. the comparison and NULLIF key by
   comparability family (22.13: numbers together, nodes with nodes, edges with
   edges, each other class with itself; null, dynamic and a deferred class are
   wildcards; opaque meets nothing), simple CASE's when list by its operand
   mask against the operand's class. exact because the rule only rules out
   pairs its push rejects: `pair_waste` shows the family key keeps 4.3M of the
   comparison's 16.9M pairs at two variables and drops no useful one.
   emission's backward passes use the same grouping, so they skip the same
   pairs; the engine's push checks the keys once more per pair, so a scan
   without the grouping (`layers`, a profile) skips them too

the check for 2 to 4 is `pruning_is_exact` (`tests/context_types.rs`): the
fixed point with a pruning off and on, every nonterminal's label set compared.
`GQL_OFF=fold`, `GQL_OFF=keys`, `GQL_OFF=fold,keys`; `GQL_SLOTS` sets the
cap; joins off, so the sets are canonical. one variable: identical for both,
81,406 labels. the probes and the generated trees (`cargo test --release
--test context_types`) pass. `--no-fold` on `gql-proof context` measures the
fold alone, `--keys` off measures the keys

## what is left, and why it is not redundancy

after the prunings the pushes at two variables are 28.4M at 1.7 µs each, and
the top of the table is list recursions: `simple case/rep1` (3.4M pushes, 95%
of its pairs useful), the comparison (3.4M, now mostly wildcard pairs: a
deferred reference against everything), `list element list` (4.4M over its
two productions). those pairs are real combinations of labels; no filter skips
them. the levers left are of three kinds

- **fewer labels**: the entries. at two variables a nonterminal holds 57 to
  188 distinct entry lists (pairs of sets), rows of a relation the join cannot
  merge when they differ in both variables. the handoff's principal typings
  with equality groups (`x = y` as one group with one set) would make most of
  those rows one label. it changes `join`, `grounds` and `within`, which
  emission from joined labels is being built on, so it waits for that
- **cheaper pushes**: three things per kept partial label are cloned (itself,
  its predecessor, the option); interning labels to ids would make the layers
  ids and the back pointers two integers. and scope's push runs once per pair
  of full labels although it depends only on the scope parts, which the
  census says are 4 to 30 times fewer than the labels: run it once per scope
  pair, and the types push once per view shape and type pair
- **parallel scans**: productions within a round are independent, and so are
  the partial labels within a scan. nothing in the engine uses the 15 cores
  yet. a round's wall time is bounded below by its largest scan, so the split
  has to go inside the hot productions, over their partial labels, with the
  layer merged after

one more measured bound, not built: emission at scope, two variables, reaches
12,686 of the 26,236 labels. a demand filter (magic sets: labels no ancestor
context takes, dropped in the fixed point) can remove at most that half, and
pushes with it; it needs an over-approximation of the reached set computed
first, so it is a separate design

## for three variables

scope alone at three variables is 70k labels, 5.2M pushes, 12 s. the typed
factor at two variables is 3.4 on labels and 37 on pushes (28.4M against
0.77M); the same factors give about 240k labels and 190M pushes, 5 to 6
minutes of fixed point in a few GB, before any of the levers above. emission
costs about five fixed points. that is a run, not a loop; parallel scans and
interning are what make it a loop

## how to run

```
# the fixed point with every pruning (joins and keys are flags, the fold is on)
gql-proof context --rule types --slots 2 --join --keys --phase fixpoint

# each pruning off, for measuring
gql-proof context --rule types --slots 1 --join --keys --phase fixpoint --no-fold
gql-proof context --rule types --slots 1 --join --phase fixpoint

# exactness: labels with and without a pruning
GQL_OFF=fold GQL_SLOTS=1 cargo test --release --test context_types \
  pruning_is_exact -- --ignored --nocapture
GQL_OFF=keys GQL_SLOTS=1 cargo test --release --test context_types \
  pruning_is_exact -- --ignored --nocapture

# where the pairs go, per production, at a cap, with joins, for one production
GQL_SLOTS=2 GQL_JOIN=1 GQL_PRODS="comparison predicate" \
  cargo test --release --test context_types pair_waste -- --ignored --nocapture

# labels split into scope part, view shape and type part
GQL_SLOTS=2 GQL_JOIN=1 cargo test --release --test context_types census \
  -- --ignored --nocapture
```

## per-target extraction (branch `tushar/prune`, later the same day)

emission was the wall at three variables (33 minutes unfinished, 2.4 million
ground searches). the proof needs one tree per target, about 6k, so
`src/context/extract.rs` reads them straight out of the fixed point: a
witness per (nonterminal, label) bottom up, a context per (nonterminal,
label) upward to the top, both memoised with tarjan-style conditional
failures across the grammar's cycles, the backward tables per production
built on first use. no grammar, no purdom, naming over the extracted nodes'
own links. `gql-proof context --phase cover --cdrc` runs it;
`common::extraction_matches_emission` (tests/common/mod.rs) checks it against
the emitted grammar on the same fixed point: productions and cdrc pairs equal
in both directions outside the lexical layer, below spelled names and among
fillable productions, and every tree passes the checkers and the rule

```
                         trees  productions  cdrc pairs  fixed point  extraction  rss
scope, cap 1              1336   1830/1830   2856/2856       0.04 s      0.3 s    63 MB
scope, cap 2              1325   1860/1860   2902/2902       0.7 s       3.3 s   117 MB
scope, cap 3              1324   1869/1869   2921/2921        12 s      ~150 s
typed, 1 var, no joins    1282   1826/1826   2840/2840        10 s      123 s    637 MB
```

(the counts are emission's and extraction's, equal; the typed row's trees
pass `verify::types` too.) two things it found on the way: `accepts` refused
every YIELD item and every WHERE beside its declaration, because `relink`
required distinct names in partial labels where the rule keeps the reference
and the declaration as two slots until finish merges them (fixed: distinct
only in finished labels, and a closed slot never shares a name with a
surviving one); and `Engine::add` moved a subsumed label's parts away, which
grounding then over-approximated (fixed: kept)

### joins, and why extraction from them is still open

with joins the fixed point is small (89k labels at two variables) but a
joined label is a product of class sets per variable, and its grounds
(every combination of single classes) include combinations no tree has:
`x = y` gives (exact, exact) and (str, str), the product adds (exact, str).
extraction over grounds tries those phantoms as candidate children, and each
one is a deep failing search before its failure is memoised: at one variable
the first target alone ran 14k witness searches and 466 wide ground searches
in 500 s. emission paid the same price (its 2.4 million ground searches and
34k failures) and purdom's productivity pass cleaned up after it. without
joins there are no phantoms and extraction is the scope path, but the typed
fixed point without joins has no fixed point after 15 minutes at two
variables (551,842 labels and rising, 1.4 GB), against 89,145 labels in 50 s
with them

the way out is the representation, not the search: a label that carries
equality groups (`x ~ y` with one set) instead of a product of sets has no
phantom for the correlations the types rule makes (comparisons, SAME,
ALL_DIFFERENT), joins stay exact and small, and extraction over grounds is
complete at the cost of the real combinations only. that is the handoff's
"principal typings with equality groups", a change to `Entry`, `Label`,
`join`, `grounds`, `within` and `meet` in `src/context/types.rs`
